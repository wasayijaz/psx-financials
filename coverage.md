## Gaps and exclusions

The intended ten-year collection is incomplete. Presence of a year does not establish that all statements or line items are available. Missing-publication counts are distinct source observations that pass the other export qualification checks but lack a verified publication date. Duplicate deliveries of the same source observation are counted once. Superseded, incomplete and rejected batches are excluded before this count. They are not estimates of all figures in unprocessed filings.
